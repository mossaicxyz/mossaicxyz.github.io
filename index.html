Why Mossaic

A marketplace that never holds your assets.

Zero custody

Listings are signed orders. NFTs and funds stay in your wallet until the trade executes on-chain.

Built on Seaport

Order matching runs on the unmodified, audited Seaport 1.6 protocol trusted across the EVM ecosystem.

Creator tools

ERC-721 and ERC-1155 collections, drops with allowlists, phased mints, reveals and ERC-2981 royalties.

Full trading

Fixed price, offers, auctions and batch purchases.

Security

Audited building blocks, locked-down keys.

  • Unmodified Seaport 1.6 core
  • Admin keys held by a 3-of-5 multisig behind a 48-hour timelock
  • Fuzz and invariant tested with Foundry, static analysis with Slither and Aderyn
  • Independent audit and bug bounty before mainnet
